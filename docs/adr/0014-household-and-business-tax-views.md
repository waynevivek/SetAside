# Keep household liability separate from freelance-attributable tax

Show total modeled household federal tax liability and the remaining balance after withholding and estimated payments separately from the incremental federal tax attributable to freelance business income. Use the business-attributable tax, not all household tax, for freelance take-home and reserve calculations. At break-even, retain the household W-2-based view while showing zero freelance take-home and reserve; loss behavior follows ADR 0013.
