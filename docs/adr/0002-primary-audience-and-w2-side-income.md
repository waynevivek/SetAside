# Primary audience and W-2 side income

SetAside's primary launch audience is full-time U.S. freelancers and creators, while also supporting W-2 employees with freelance side income. The first screen stays focused on the freelance business; optional, collapsed W-2 details let side hustlers account for combined household income and wage withholding without turning the product into comprehensive tax-preparation software. Investment and rental income and detailed credits remain outside v1.
