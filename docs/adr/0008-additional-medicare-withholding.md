# Account for Additional Medicare Tax withheld from W-2 wages

The optional W-2 section collects Box 6 totals for the taxpayer and, for Married Filing Jointly, the spouse. The estimator uses Box 5 and Box 6 to identify Additional Medicare Tax withheld and credits that amount when calculating the remaining federal balance. If Box 6 is entered while Box 5 is blank or zero for that person, display a validation warning for that person and exclude that person's Box 6 from Additional Medicare Tax withholding reconciliation without blocking the rest of the calculation. Keep the inputs collapsed by default and explain Box 6 in plain language.
